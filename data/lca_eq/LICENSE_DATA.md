# LCA-EQ data rights and attribution

The repository-level MIT license covers LGDNet source code only. It does not
grant rights in satellite imagery or override the terms of the original
imagery providers.

## Dataset components

- Author-prepared land-cover and visible-damage labels, event metadata, split
  manifests, and AOI metadata are released under Creative Commons
  Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International (CC BY-NC 4.0), with attribution
  to the LCA-EQ authors and citation of the associated paper and versioned
  dataset record. The license text is at
  <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/>. This license does not
  grant rights in third-party RGB imagery.
- Maxar Open Data Program imagery is governed by CC BY-NC 4.0 only when the
  particular source asset is covered by that program and its license. Users
  must preserve the source attribution and comply with the applicable terms.
- Yangbi RGB imagery was accessed through Google Earth historical imagery and
  is not redistributed in this GitHub repository. This project does not grant a
  license to copy, download in bulk, or redistribute Google Earth imagery.
  Users must obtain any imagery through lawful access and follow Google's
  applicable terms and attribution requirements.

The Zenodo v1.1 archive includes 183 Yangbi RGB patches accessed through Google
Earth historical imagery. The authors' use of these images is for
non-commercial academic research and follows applicable Google Earth terms and
attribution requirements. This statement does not grant a separate license or
permission to redistribute or reuse those pixels. They are not covered by
CC BY-NC 4.0, and any further use remains subject to Google's applicable terms.
See the [Zenodo v1.1 record](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23934838).

The previously published Zenodo v1.0 record uses CC BY 4.0. That record is
historical; its license does not override third-party image-provider rights.
The corrected versioned record must use the same component-level distinction
and must not represent any author-selected license as applying to Google Earth
pixels or other third-party material whose rights are not held by the authors.

## Attribution

For Maxar imagery covered by the Open Data Program, cite the relevant Maxar
Open Data event/source asset and retain any attribution supplied with that
asset. For Google Earth imagery, follow Google's current attribution rules when
lawfully displaying or using it. Cite the LCA-EQ paper and the specific Zenodo
dataset version when using the annotations, metadata, or split files.
